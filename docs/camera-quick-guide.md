# Camera quick guide

1. Import artwork in **Arrange** and position the layers.
2. Open **Camera Movements**, expand **Basic Cinematic Moves**, choose a move, enter its start, duration and coordinates or scale, then select **Add movement**. Use **Coordinate picker** and hover over the preview to see coordinates; click a point, then choose **Use picked point**.
3. Play or scrub to preview. In **Timeline**, edit movement start/end times or use Edit and Delete. Select **Save** to keep changes.

Coordinates are scene pixels from the top-left. Pan changes X, tilt changes Y, and push moves toward an X/Y point while enlarging the composition. Zoom enlarges or reduces the composition without changing the viewpoint. All new moves affect the complete composition equally, independent of layer depth, and progress at a constant rate.

Flat images cannot show a true perspective-changing dolly; push approximates it with camera translation and scaling. **Static / locked-off** clears camera movement after confirmation. See [Camera movements](animation.md) for combinations and legacy project behavior.
