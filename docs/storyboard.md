# Scene 01 — The arrival

**Historical storyboard for the legacy preview**, not the current StoryStudio camera proof or editor. See the [README](../README.md) for current features.

Working title: **A world before his time**. Duration: 24 seconds, 576 frames at 24 fps.

## Editorial choice

The illustration belongs to the jungle-arrival passage, not the castle prologue. This scene begins after the time jump. The PDF's “Then silence” gives the opening a clean audio/visual reset. A future castle/crystal prologue can be placed before it without rebuilding this scene.

## Animation limits

The character is a single cutout: a 0.18%-amplitude breathing scale, not joint articulation. The foreground has sub-degree rotation. No head turns, blinking, lip sync, or simulated moving water are claimed. Those need extra masks, meshes, or separately painted parts.

Camera motion is weighted by layer depth and deterministic. A fixed seed makes particles reproducible. All motion is evaluated from absolute scene time, so scrubbing, preview, and offline rendering agree exactly.

## Audio

No narration was supplied or generated. Exported videos are silent. The studio has an optional low-volume synthesized noise bed for preview, muted by default; it is not a natural field recording. A later production pass can add voice-over, forest ambience, a landing impact, and a distant creature call after the final line.
